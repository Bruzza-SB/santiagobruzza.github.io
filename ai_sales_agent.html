AI Sales & Lead-Qualification Agent

A public chatbot for my consulting practice that runs first-contact discovery with prospects, records structured notes and books the meeting in the chat. Anything exposed to the public internet needs to be safe from abuse and have predictable costs, so both were designed in from day one.

Cost governance

  1. 01
    Prompt cachingThe stable system context is cached.
  2. 02
    Zero-token intakeForms instead of LLM turns for basic data.
  3. 03
    Tiered model routingEach turn is routed between Claude Haiku and Claude Sonnet.
  4. 04
    Per-session token budgetsCaps the cost of any single conversation.

    Daily / monthly spend capsAn automatic kill switch makes costs predictable.
