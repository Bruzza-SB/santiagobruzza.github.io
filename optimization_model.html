Supply Chain Clustering & Route Optimization

A Canadian food manufacturer wanted to minimize the total operating cost of its distribution network and decide which distribution center should supply which store.

01Data

  • 8 DCs and 3,500 stores with latitude and longitude
  • Costs per DC from the P&L: gasoline, truck depreciation, driver salary, loading
  • Monthly demand per store

02Approach

  1. ClusterK-Means on store coordinates; the number of clusters is set by truck limits (km and driving time).
  2. DistancesStore↔store and store↔DC driving distance and time from the OSRM API.
  3. RouteTraveling Salesman Problem solved with Google OR-Tools.
  4. Cost-to-serveFuel, truck amortization, load/unload time and driver salary per unit × demand → trip and total cost.
  5. OptimizeA cost-minimization model (built by a specialized optimization team) assigns DCs to stores.
