AI Sales Call Auditor

Outsourced sales agents make thousands of calls, but one human auditor can only listen to a few. Requested by the Sales Director, I took it from proof of concept to production: a pipeline that transcribes the calls every night, scores each one against the sales scorecard with an LLM, and shows the results from a global overview down to the single call.

01Problem

The sales team works with 5 outsourced vendors whose agents call prospects (acquisition) and also call existing customers to offer other products (upselling). Every call follows guidelines: a cordial greeting, validating the customer's data, not interrupting, discovery questions about the customer's needs, a clear offer of the products, and more.

A sales employee listened to a sample of calls from each vendor to check compliance. With an average call of about 15 minutes, only a very small share of the calls could ever be reviewed.

The Sales Director asked for an automatic audit. Only the internal sales team can open the results, and it decides what, if anything, to share with the vendors.

Before

  • One person manually audited about 100 calls a month
  • About 15 minutes of audio per audited call
  • Most calls never reviewed

After

  • Every recorded call is transcribed and audited by AI, at about US$0.04 per call
  • Score, errors and verdict for each call, with a comment per error
  • Results by vendor, supervisor, agent and campaign

02The scorecard

The auditor applies the same rules the human auditor uses. Each evaluated aspect lists critical and non-critical errors.

  1. 01
    100 points for a perfect callEvery non-critical error deducts a set number of points.
  2. 02
    85 points to passBelow the minimum, the call is rejected.
  3. 03
    Critical errors fail the callAny critical error rejects the call automatically, whatever the score.
  4. 04
    Explained verdictA rejected call states the reason it was rejected.
  5. 05
    Subjective criteria left outSome criteria, such as interrupting the customer, cannot be judged from a transcript alone. Together with the sales team I excluded them from the AI audit.

The full scorecard is embedded in the Gemini prompt, and the output is constrained by a JSON schema so every call returns the same structured fields.

04Dashboard

The first screen is a global overview of all audited calls: trend charts over time, histograms of call scores, and KPIs for calls audited, approved, rejected, sales closed and average score.

From there users drill down along the chain of responsibility. A vendor has several supervisors and each supervisor manages several agents, so performance is visible at every level.

  1. 01
    VendorCompare the outsourced providers.
  2. 02
    SupervisorSee how each team performs.
  3. 03
    AgentSpot individual strengths and gaps.
  4. 04
    CampaignThe same KPIs by calling campaign.
  5. 05
    Single callOpen any call to read the score, each error and its comment, and the verdict.

05How it was built

I built it alone in about a month, working with the infrastructure team, which stood up the transcription server.

  1. Proof of conceptA small set of calls, used to show the scope and the app to the sales team before scaling.
  2. Capacity planningWorking sessions with infrastructure to estimate expected usage, load and response times of the GPU transcription service.
  3. Off-peak schedulingThe recordings platform can only be read outside working hours, so transcription runs overnight.
  4. ProductionThe full nightly pipeline and the dashboard, deployed as a Cloud Run service.

06Validation & production readiness

Three things had to be settled before the auditor could run on real calls every night.

Validation note. There was no hand-audited sample with the same level of detail as the AI audit, so the sales team spot-checked AI evaluations against manual listening to confirm they were coherent.

  1. 01
    ValidationRules a transcript cannot capture, such as subjective criteria, were excluded from the AI audit.
  2. 02
    Off-peak accessTranscription scheduled from 23:00 to 07:00.
  3. 03
    Sizing the GPU serviceUsage and response-time estimates agreed with infrastructure.

07Security & access

  1. 01
    Card data maskedCredit-card numbers are hashed before transcripts are stored.
  2. 02
    Group-restricted accessOnly members of a dedicated Google Cloud user group can open the app on Cloud Run.
  3. 03
    On-premise transcriptionSpeech-to-text runs on a GPU in the company's own data center.

08Impact

  • Quality control moves from about 100 manually audited calls a month to auditing the recorded calls at scale, at about US$0.04 per call
  • Every rejected call comes with a clear reason
  • Customer payment data is protected through masking
  • Vendor, supervisor, agent and campaign performance is visible in one place
  • The human auditor can focus on the cases that need judgment
  • The sales team gets a basis for conversations with the supervisors of agents who make many critical errors; follow-up actions stay with the sales team
