Customer Comments: Topics & Sentiment

Every time a customer contacts the company they get a satisfaction survey that ends with a free-text question. The Customer Intelligence team wanted that feedback analyzed automatically, every month.

  • NLP
  • TF-IDF
  • NLTK
  • K-Means
  • Sentiment analysis

01Approach

  1. CleanTokenize, lowercase, remove stopwords and punctuation, lemmatize (NLTK).
  2. VectorizeTF-IDF matrix, plus features flagging words close to business terms such as “price” or “invoice”.
  3. ClusterK-Means with 5 clusters agreed with Customer Intelligence.
  4. ScorePositive / neutral / negative with pysentiment.
  5. StoreResults saved to the database and joined with customer data.

03Impact

Complete, automated analysis of survey feedback, ready to cross with customer data to drive improvements in service and customer care.
