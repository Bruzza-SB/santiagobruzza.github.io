Merchant Name Unifier

The same merchant appears in transactions under many names: “Amazon US”, “Amazon MX”, store numbers, random codes. Without one name per merchant, market-share analysis and merchant-level products are impossible.

02Approach

  1. CleanRegex removes special characters and random alphanumeric strings, and normalizes case.
  2. EmbedA Hugging Face embedding model turns each name into a vector.
  3. ClusterDBSCAN groups names that mean the same merchant.
  4. NameAll n-grams per cluster are generated, and the most frequent and longest one becomes the unified name.

03Impact

  • A unique merchant catalog
  • Analyses that reflect true merchant market share
  • Enables features such as credit cards restricted to specific merchants
