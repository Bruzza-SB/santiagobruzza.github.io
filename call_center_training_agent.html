Call Center Training Chatbot with RAG

The knowledge needed to train call center agents was spread across 30+ PowerPoint decks full of text, tables and images. I turned it into one conversational, searchable source.

01Problem

Training content lived in 30+ presentations. Finding an answer meant opening decks one by one, and a lot of the knowledge was inside images and tables that plain text search cannot read.

02Approach

  1. Extractpython-pptx pulls text, tables and images from every deck into structured JSON.
  2. Describe imagesImages are Base64-encoded and described by a multimodal model through the Gemini API.
  3. MergeThe descriptions are written back into the JSON next to their slide.
  4. IndexThe corpus is created in Vertex AI as the chatbot's knowledge source.
  5. ChatAgents ask questions and get answers grounded in the training material (RAG).

03Impact

  • A centralized, searchable resource replacing 30+ scattered training documents
  • Knowledge hidden in images and tables becomes searchable
  • Agents get conversational access to training content
